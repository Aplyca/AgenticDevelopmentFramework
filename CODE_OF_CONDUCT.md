# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct. Everyone who participates — in issues, pull requests, discussions, or any other project space — is expected to follow it.

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, welcome people of every background and experience level, and focus criticism on ideas and work rather than people. Harassment, personal attacks, and discriminatory language are not tolerated.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates the code of conduct, email **dev@aplyca.com**. Reports are handled confidentially by the project maintainers, who will review each one and respond in line with the Contributor Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
