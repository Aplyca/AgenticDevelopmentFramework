# A stakeholder's reply that can be read more than one way

`/adf:triage` reads this at step 1, when the task is a reply on the tracker — to an update, a
question, or delivered work — and it can reasonably mean more than one thing.

Don't pick a reading. "Looks great, can we have it on the homepage too?" may ask for new work, or
only whether it's possible. List the readings, each with what it costs: no change, a change request
on the spec, or new work. Recommend one, and draft a short reply that confirms it, for the developer
to approve; carry into it every question the reply left unanswered. Build nothing until the reading
is confirmed: until then the deliverable is that reply.

## Rationalizations (do not accept these)

| Agent says... | Why it's wrong |
|---|---|
| "The reply most likely means this, so I'll start on it" | A wrong reading builds the wrong thing, or reopens delivered work nobody asked to change. List the readings and their cost, recommend one, and confirm it with the requester first. |

## Red flags (stop and reassess)

- A stakeholder's reply you can read two ways, and you've started building on one of them.
